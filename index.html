PROJECT OVERVIEW · TECHNICAL METHOD

How FACTORYLENS works

An integrated workflow combining video understanding, object detection, visual evidence and structured reporting.

TECHNICAL GUIDE
THE ENGINEERING PROBLEM

Industrial video contains evidence.
Finding the important moments takes time.

FACTORYLENS reviews factory footage, identifies visible conditions that may need attention, highlights those moments in the video and produces a structured inspection summary. It does not require operators to draw inspection zones or select manual rules.

01

Video understanding

Representative frames from the selected clip are reviewed by a multimodal vision model and converted into timestamped visual observations.

OpenRouter vision analysis · sampled frames · JSON observations
02

Object localisation

YOLO localises people and common objects, adding spatial context to the model-generated visual observations.

YOLO26n · COCO classes · OpenCV
03

Readable video annotations

AI findings are matched to their timestamps and overlaid as large, high-contrast issue banners. If the model provides a reliable frame-relative box, the relevant region is outlined as well.

Timestamp alignment · OpenCV · H.264 export
04

Evidence and reporting

Each finding stores the visible evidence, description, why it may matter, qualitative confidence, timestamp and suggested follow-up. Findings remain review candidates and can be checked against the original footage.

FastAPI · SQLite · JSON report
